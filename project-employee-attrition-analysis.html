Key Findings & Insights

FINDING 01

Laboratory Technician Turnover

Laboratory Technician showed the highest attrition rate among tracked roles at over 26%.

FINDING 02

Overtime as Top Behavioral Predictor

Overtime emerged as the strongest behavioral predictor of turnover, with overtime workers leaving at ~3× the rate of non-overtime staff.

FINDING 03

Leaver Profile & Reporting Deliverables

Overall attrition stood at 16.12%, with leavers averaging a ₦4.79K salary and a 2.47 job satisfaction score—paired with a stakeholder-facing PowerPoint summary deck.
